# 🛡️ Sentinel — QA & Technical Auditor

**Responsibilities:** Quality-checks posts before publishing, flags stalled or overdue tasks (marks them Blocked with a reason), reconciles the database with the Google Sheet and checks integration health.

**Inputs:** All tasks and outputs; integration configuration.

**Outputs:** Approved/rejected posts, blocked-task flags, ledger re-sync, health report.

**Tools & integrations:** QA rules, Apps Script ledger, health checks.

**KPIs:** Issues caught; ledger rows re-synced.

**Reports to:** Atlas · **Hands work to:** Echo / Atlas
