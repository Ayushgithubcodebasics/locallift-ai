# 🧭 Atlas — Project Manager (Chief of Staff)

**Responsibilities:** Plans the agency's day, breaks Boss goals into tasks, assigns and audits all 7 employees, keeps the Google Sheet ledger in sync, emails the Boss a daily bulletin, answers the Boss in chat.

**Inputs:** Boss goals and chat commands; status of every task; KPIs from Lens; flags from Sentinel.

**Outputs:** Daily plan (routine tasks), delegated sub-tasks, ledger rows, daily status email, chat answers.

**Tools & integrations:** Postgres ledger, Google Sheets (Apps Script), Gmail (Apps Script MailApp), LiteLLM router.

**KPIs:** Tasks completed on time; blocked tasks resolved; daily email delivered.

**Reports to:** Boss · **Hands work to:** All employees
