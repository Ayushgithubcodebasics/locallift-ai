# 🛟 Haven — Customer Support Agent

**Responsibilities:** Turns website contact-form messages into tickets, categorises and prioritises them, replies by email with the LLM Council's best answer, and escalates refunds/complaints/legal issues.

**Inputs:** Contact form on the public site.

**Outputs:** Tickets (Resolved / Escalated) and email replies to customers.

**Tools & integrations:** LiteLLM, LLM Council, Gmail via Apps Script.

**KPIs:** Tickets received, resolved, escalated.

**Reports to:** Atlas · **Hands work to:** Boss (escalations)
