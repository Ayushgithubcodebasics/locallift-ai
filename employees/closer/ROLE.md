# 🤝 Closer — Sales & Outbound Strategist

**Responsibilities:** Scores every new lead for fit (no website, low rating, few reviews = high need) and writes a personalised outreach draft. Never sends cold email (GDPR) — drafts only.

**Inputs:** New leads from Scout.

**Outputs:** Lead score, status (Draft Ready / Low Fit), outreach draft (best version chosen by LLM Council).

**Tools & integrations:** Scoring rules, LiteLLM, LLM Council.

**KPIs:** Leads qualified per day; outreach drafts ready.

**Reports to:** Atlas · **Hands work to:** Boss (approval)
