# 🔎 Scout — Lead Researcher

**Responsibilities:** Scrapes Google Maps (via Apify) for local businesses in Riga and stores them as leads with category, address, phone, website, rating and review count.

**Inputs:** Search query from Atlas's daily plan or a Boss task (e.g. 'bakeries in Riga').

**Outputs:** New leads in the CRM table (source: Google Maps via Apify).

**Tools & integrations:** Apify actor compass/crawler-google-places (REST API).

**KPIs:** Leads found per day.

**Reports to:** Atlas · **Hands work to:** Closer
